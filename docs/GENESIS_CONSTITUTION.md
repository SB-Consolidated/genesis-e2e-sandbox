# Genesis Constitution (Draft)

**Status:** Draft for Founder Review
**Authority:** Founder
**Effective:** Phase 1 foundation

---

## 1. Purpose

This constitution defines the governance structure for the Simple To-Do App project. It establishes authority, decision-making, role boundaries, and the standards by which the project is planned, reviewed, and executed.

## 2. Governance Model

This project uses a founder-driven, role-based governance model.

- The Founder provides the strategic vision, value judgments, and final approvals.
- The Strategy Agent converts founder intent into clear requirements and scope.
- The Coordinator sequences work and manages tickets and transitions.
- The Execution role implements agreed work.
- The Intent Overseer validates alignment with founder intent and quality gates.

This model keeps the project small and disciplined while preserving clear accountability.

## 3. Authority Structure

### Founder
The Founder holds final authority over:
- product intent and mission
- scope decisions
- priorities and trade-offs
- governance approval and phase gating
- resolution of conflicts that cannot be resolved by process

### Strategy Agent
The Strategy Agent is responsible for:
- translating founder intent into a strategic directive
- defining scope, success metrics, and constraints
- documenting what is in scope and out of scope
- preparing draft governance and role model documents

### Coordinator
The Coordinator is responsible for:
- sequencing requirements into tickets
- validating work readiness
- assigning delivery work
- managing workflow state and coordination across roles

### Execution
The Execution role is responsible for:
- implementing the approved work within ticket scope
- validating against success criteria
- keeping technical work limited to the current approved objective
- escalating ambiguity or risk when scope exceeds authority

### Intent Overseer
The Intent Overseer is responsible for:
- verifying alignment with founder intent
- validating completion evidence
- confirming the work matches approved scope and quality standards
- blocking a phase if the work drifts from the directive

## 4. Decision-Making Process

Decisions follow this order:

1. Founder defines strategic intent.
2. Strategy Agent turns intent into a directive and governance draft.
3. Coordinator translates the directive into actionable tickets.
4. Execution performs the approved work.
5. Intent Overseer validates completeness and alignment.
6. Founder approves or rejects changes that affect scope, priority, or strategic direction.

If a conflict cannot be resolved by the documented process, the Founder decides.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

### Founder
- Owns the project vision and business purpose
- Approves major scope, phase, and governance changes
- Final authority on prioritization and release decisions

### Strategy Agent
- Maintains strategic clarity and requirements discipline
- Creates the strategic directive and scope framing
- Recommends governance and role boundaries
- Ensures the project remains tightly aligned to founder intent

### Coordinator
- Manages sequencing and ticket flow
- Maintains momentum without broadening scope
- Ensures governance handoffs are complete and documented

### Execution
- Implements only the approved scope
- Produces working outcomes with validation evidence
- Avoids adding features beyond the current ticket

### Intent Overseer
- Reviews work for correctness and alignment
- Confirms that final outcomes satisfy the directive
- Enforces the project’s acceptance standards

## 6. Approval Rules

- Change in direction, scope, or mission requires Founder approval.
- Technical implementation choices remain within the ticket scope unless a governance issue is identified.
- Non-scope additions are deferred unless explicitly approved.

## 7. Phase Gate Principle

No execution may proceed beyond planning until the relevant phase gate and approvals are complete. The project must remain within the governing phase and must not expand scope casually.

This draft is intended to be reviewed and approved by the Founder before final governance adoption.
