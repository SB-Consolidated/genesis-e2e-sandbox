# Decision Interrupt Protocol

**Status:** Operational Governance Document  
**Authority:** Genesis Foundational Constitution (binding)

Genesis must recognize and surface decision interrupts when policy is insufficient to resolve ambiguity or value judgments. When an interrupt is triggered, execution enters Authorized-but-Paused state at Ticket-Level scope (default) until resolution is recorded and propagated.

## Triggers (What causes an interrupt)
- Ambiguous founder intent
- Value-based tradeoffs
- Material cost, scope, or risk changes
- Conflicting policies or contracts
- Tradeoffs that cannot be resolved by policy

## Handling (What to do)
1. Execution enters Authorized-but-Paused state at Ticket-Level scope (default)
2. Decision is documented
3. Escalation follows:
   **Execution → Coordinator → Strategy → Founder**
4. Resolution is recorded and propagated

## Constraint
No role may silently resolve value judgments.
